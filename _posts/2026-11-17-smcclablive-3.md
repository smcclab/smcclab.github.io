SMCClablive #3 is the third in the ANU Sound, Music, and Creative Computing
Lab's research performance series featuring new instruments, new sounds, and
new artistic experiences on stage.

This time we present new music for computer and radio: works to be presented
at the [Australasian Computer Music Conference](https://computermusic.org.au)
in December 2026, and works in recent development in the lab. The performers
are [Charles Martin](https://charlesmartin.au), [Alexander
Hunter](https://alexanderhunter.com.au), and Albert-Ngabo Niyonsenga.

Join us for this lunchtime concert of experimental computer music!

## Program

1. [_Hyphae_](https://charlesmartin.au/projects/hyphae/): Charles Martin + Alexander Hunter
2. _Bad Microphone 2_: Albert-Ngabo Niyonsenga + Charles Martin
3. _Agentier_: Charles Martin

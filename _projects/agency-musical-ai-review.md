Researchers describe AI music systems using many competing ideas of agency: mixed-initiative interaction, co-creativity, human-AI teaming, material agency, and agential realism. These frames lead to very different design decisions, but they are rarely compared. In this project you'll conduct a structured review of how agency is defined and used in musical AI and intelligent instrument research (e.g., in NIME, CHI, and ICCC proceedings), and propose a framework for how the concepts relate. You can build on the lab's experience running a scoping review (of 199 studies on interactive sonification). The coded corpus you produce is released openly as part of your artefact.

- **Summer or one semester:** a structured review of a bounded corpus (e.g., NIME papers from 2015 onwards that discuss agency), with a map of agency concepts and where they're used.
- **Two semesters or Master:** a full scoping review protocol, and a proposed conceptual framework tested by applying it to existing intelligent instruments.
